How to play

  1. Tap a screw to take it out. It flies into the box of its color at the top.
  2. Only screws with nothing in front of them come out. If a board covers a screw, take that board off first.
  3. A board falls off when its last screw is out.
  4. Each box holds 3 screws. When it’s full, the next box comes in.
  5. No box for that color yet? The screw waits in the tray (5 spots) and hops into its box when one shows up.
  6. Tray full and nothing fits a box = stuck. Tap + Box (a quick ad) for a 4th or 5th box.
  7. Drag to turn it (a house, the Arch, a bus...) and find screws on the other sides. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in.
