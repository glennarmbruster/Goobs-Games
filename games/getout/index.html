How to play

  1. Look around. Swipe left or right, or tap the side arrows, to turn to another wall.
  2. Tap things to look closer. The arrow at the bottom steps back out.
  3. Pick things up by tapping them. They go in your bag at the bottom.
  4. Use an item: tap it in your bag, then tap where it should go.
  5. Combine items: drag one item onto another in your bag.
  6. Inspect: tap a selected item again to look at it closely and turn it over. Things hide clues on the back!
  7. Notebook: clues you find are written down for you. Tap Snapshot to save a picture of what you see.
  8. Stuck? The light bulb gives a nudge, then a clearer hint, then the exact next step. Fewer hints and a quicker escape earn more stars.
  9. Easy, Medium, Hard: the same room with fewer or more steps. Codes and clues change every new game.
