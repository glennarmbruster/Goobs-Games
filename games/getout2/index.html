How to play

  1. You’re the stunt double. Mortimer Mayhem films every stunt for real. Get out of each scene in one piece.
  2. Look around. Swipe left or right, or tap the side arrows, to turn. Tap things to look closer; the arrow at the bottom steps back out.
  3. Pick things up by tapping them. Tap an item in your bag, then tap where it should go. Drag one item onto another to combine them. Tap a selected item again to look at it and turn it over.
  4. Careful! Some things are deadly. The scene always warns you somehow: a sign, a label, a note, a wire. Read before you poke.
  5. If you don’t make it: a quick cartoon, then Try again puts you back just before it went wrong. Every way to die you find goes in your Stats.
  6. Against the clock: once a trap starts you can see it coming. The clock stops while a sheet or the Menu is open.
  7. Notebook: clues are written down for you. Tap Snapshot to save a picture.
  8. Stuck? The light bulb gives a nudge, then a clearer hint, then the exact next step. Hints never lead you into a trap, and on Easy and Medium they warn you about the next one.
  9. Stars: no deaths, no hints (free on Easy) and a quick finish earn all three.
  10. Easy, Medium, Hard: the same scene with fewer or more steps; Hard has red herrings and a shorter clock. Codes and clues change every new game.
