How to play

  1. Tap the front blaster in any of the 3 lines. It moves up into a slot.
  2. Blasters shoot cubes of their own color that they can see from the front (the yellow edge). One shot knocks off one cube.
  3. The number is how many shots it has left. When it runs out, it leaves and the slot opens up.
  4. If none of its color can be hit, it waits in its slot (you’ll see zzz). Knock off the cubes in front to uncover more.
  5. When all 5 slots are full and nobody can shoot, the corral’s jammed. Watch a quick ad for an extra slot, or try again.
  6. Drag the sculpture to turn it and look around. Bigger ones have surprise colors inside.
